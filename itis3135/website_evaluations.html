Website Evaluations

Awwwards Websites

A showcase of beautifully designed and innovative websites, featuring clean layouts, consistent typography, and modern user interfaces.

  • Contrast: Excellent use of color contrast, typography hierarchy, and spacing to separate content and guide the user’s eye.
  • Repetition: Consistent layout patterns and navigation structures create a cohesive experience.
  • Alignment: Perfectly aligned text, images, and grid-based layouts provide a clean, orderly appearance.
  • Proximity: Related items are clearly grouped together — captions under images, menus near relevant content.

Ling's Cars

Notorious for its chaotic, overstimulating design filled with clashing colors, inconsistent fonts, and disorganized content.

  • Contrast: Poor — clashing colors everywhere make it hard to focus or read content comfortably.
  • Repetition: Virtually none — every section looks and feels different, making the experience disjointed.
  • Alignment: Inconsistent — elements are scattered with little visual connection or grid system.
  • Proximity: Weak — unrelated content is mixed together, and logical grouping is mostly absent.
